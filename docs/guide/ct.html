EDNiX · CT

Registering a CT so that the PET can follow it to the anatomy and the templates

Three steps, one goal: a reliable CT → anatomy (or CT → template) registration record for the PET pipeline.

Source-of-truth rule: checked against the EDNiX repository (modalities/ct, modalities/pet, Tools/getpath.py, master, 13 November 2026).

What the CT pipeline is for

The CT pipeline exists to serve the PET pipeline. It registers a CT (in Hounsfield units) to the subject’s anatomy, to a CT template or to a CT study template, and writes a registration record that the PET pipeline then follows (PET → CT → anatomy or template).

FILE = concrete on-disk file/patternSETTING = launcher parameterRECORD = JSON registration record
Run order: ANAT pipeline (if the subjects have an MRI) → CT pipeline → PET pipeline with IhaveaCT=True. Use the same animalPosition/humanPosition as the PET of the same PET/CT acquisition, and the same type_norm, creat_study_template, coregistration_longitudinal as the anatomical launcher.
Before registering a CT: a CT acquired with a gantry tilt must be resampled at DICOM conversion (dcm2niix does it, Li et al. 2016; GantryDetectorTilt in the sidecar), otherwise the image is sheared and no rigid transform will fix it. Overlay the reoriented CT and the T1w (Need_check_orient=True): a flipped axis comes from the headers, not from the metric.

Which target does the CT use?

Chosen in the launcher; one CT per session (the first one if several, with a warning).

Same scanner space as the anatomy

No registration: the CT receives the world transform of the anatomy’s reorientation and follows its AC-PC matrix.

IhaveanANAT=True, anat_ct_same_space=True

1v2b · MRI, other space

Rigid CT → anatomy (AC-PC head image), multi-start, then the anatomy → template chains of the ANAT pipeline.

IhaveanANAT=True

2v1 · CT template, no MRI

CT → CT template already in the reference space (type_of_transform_template, SyNRA by default).

IhaveanANAT=False, template_CT='…'

2v2 · no MRI, no CT template

CTs restricted to the head build a CT study template (Step 2); each CT is registered to it. The PET pipeline’s bridge step then links it to the reference template through an FDG study template.

IhaveanANAT=False, creat_study_template_ct=True
With no anatomy, no CT template and no CT study template, the launcher stops: there is nothing to register the CT to.

How EDNiX creates those outputs

  1. Positions come from the launcher, else PatientPosition of the sidecar.
  2. deoblique='WARP' resamples an oblique CT on a cardinal grid (3dWarp, as the anatomy); 'header' drops the obliquity.
  3. In the scanner space of the anatomy, the CT receives the world transform of the anatomy’s reorientation (reference_world_map), recorded in its sidecar so that the PET of the PET/CT can follow it.

What should I inspect?

Need_check_orient=True opens freeview over the anatomy.

Why this stage exists

Without anatomy or CT template, CTs of the cohort are brought to a common CT space so that one bridge to the reference template serves every subject.

Reads / prerequisites
FILEreoriented CT of every sessionFrom Step 1.
SETTINGstudyT_transform'SyNRA'.
Creates / changes
FILEct/orig/preprocessing/forMC/<base>_desc-head_ct.nii.gzCT restricted to the head.
FILE<bids_dir>/sty_template/ct/…CT study template and its diary.
FILEct/orig/matrices/<base>_to_studyTemplate_*Session → study template transforms.

How EDNiX creates those outputs

  1. Head image: head mask = voxels > −500 HU, largest connected component (removes the bed and head holder), holes filled, dilated 5 mm; outside set to −1000 HU; image cropped to the box of the mask (world coordinates kept, so transforms apply to the full CT).
  2. Head images are averaged and iteratively registered (SyNRA: rigid + affine + SyN).

What should I inspect?

A CT template has no brain contrast; it is linked to atlases only by the PET bridge step.

modalities/ct/coreg2anat.py (uses modalities/pet/coreg2anat.rigid_to)
Core stage
Why this stage exists

Links the CT to every target space and writes the record that the PET pipeline composes with its own PET → CT step.

Reads / prerequisites
FILEreoriented CTMoving image.
FILEanatomy (AC-PC head image, brain, mask) + backtonative packages1v2b / same space.
FILEtemplate_CT or CT study template2v1 / 2v2.
SETTINGcoreg_init, coreg_transform, coreg_metric, ct_brain_refine, do_manual'auto', 'Rigid', 'mattes', False, False.
Creates / changes
RECORDct/orig/matrices/<base>_desc-registration_ct.jsonScenario, targets, chains to/from the CT; read by the PET pipeline.
FILEct/orig/matrices/*candidates.jsonScore of every start.
FILEct/orig/preprocessing/forMC/*_desc-intracranial_mask.nii.gzIntracranial cavity of the CT.
FILEct/acpc/…_space-acpc_ct, ct/templates/<name>/…_space-<name>_ctCT in each target space (linear).
FILEct/orig/preprocessing/<base>_space-orig_<T_CT>.nii.gzAnatomy in CT space (for viewing).
FILEct/QC/<base>_space-<target>_ct.pngCT with the target’s brain mask as contour; copies in <bids_dir>/QC/ct_in_acpc, ct_in_template.

How EDNiX creates those outputs

  1. The CT is shifted to ≥ 0 and restricted to its head (> −500 HU, largest component). Mutual information is the reference metric for CT–MRI (West et al. 1997).
  2. Multi-start (coreg_init='auto'): centres of mass of the masked images, centres of the fields of view, the anatomy’s AC-PC matrix, identity, a rotation search (15° steps within ±90°, recovers an 80° pitch within 0.5 mm on the phantom) and, for a CT, the centroids of the upper brain of the intracranial cavity (bone > 300 HU, closed 2 mm, filled slice by slice) and of the anatomy’s brain. With neck and shoulders in the field of view, centres of mass are pulled to the body; the brain start gives 0.7 mm on that phantom. The start with the highest Mattes MI covering 90 % of the brain is kept.
  3. coreg_init='acpc' alone works only when CT and anatomy share scanner coordinates.
  4. Optional brain window (ct_brain_refine=True): on [−1000, 2000] HU the parenchyma (CSF ≈ 5, WM ≈ 30, GM ≈ 40 HU) falls in one or two of the 32 histogram bins, so skull and scalp drive the metric. The second pass registers the CT in a 0–80 HU window (1-mm smoothing, bone > 200 HU set to 0) inside the anatomy’s brain dilated 3 mm (Muschelli et al. 2015). Neutral on phantoms; try it when the QC shows the brain shifted inside a well-aligned skull.
  5. With an anatomy, the CT → AC-PC transform is composed with the anatomy → template chains (backtonative.get) for every template target. 2v1: CT → CT template with type_of_transform_template; 2v2: reuses Step 2.

What should I inspect?

Look at ct_in_acpc first. Rigid CT–MRI registration by MI is accurate to about 1–2 mm in the human head (West et al. 1997; Studholme et al. 1999); a larger error usually comes from the headers or a wrong start, not from the metric.

What happens next? The PET pipeline, with IhaveaCT=True.

QC and resets

  • ct/QC/<base>_space-<target>_ct.png and <bids_dir>/QC/ct_in_acpc, ct_in_template; the QC tab of the interface shows them.
  • ct/orig/matrices/*candidates.json: if the kept start is not the one you expect (for example com with the body in the field of view), rerun with coreg_init='brain' or 'search'.
  • modalities/ct/_000_Data_Reset removes EDNiX outputs under ct/, keeps raw BIDS files and manual crops; RESET_KEEP_REGISTRATION=True keeps orig/matrices.
  • run_all_ednix.py runs CT after anat and before PET for each BIDS folder.
