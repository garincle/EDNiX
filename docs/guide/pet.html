EDNiX · PET

From raw PET frames to SUV, ratios, kinetic parameters and template-space images

This page follows a PET session from the BIDS file to the exported parametric images, for a beginner who needs to know what happens to their data and for a developer who needs to know which module writes which file.

Source-of-truth rule: stage order, filenames and routing were checked against the EDNiX repository (modalities/pet, Tools/getpath.py, master, 13 November 2026). Every parameter is listed in the PET parameter reference.

How to read this pipeline

Each stage uses the same structure as the ANAT and fMRI guides: why it exists, what it reads, what it creates, how, what to inspect, and what comes next.

FILE = concrete on-disk file/patternSETTING = launcher parameterSTATE = in-memory routingRECORD = JSON registration record
One principle explains most design choices: every computation (motion correction, parametric images, regional time-activity curves, kinetic models) is done in the PET’s own space, orig. Atlases and masks are brought into the PET (one resampling of a label image), and only the final parametric images are resampled to the anatomy’s AC-PC space and to the template spaces. Dynamic data are therefore never resampled more than once after motion correction, and regional curves are computed on measured voxels.
Names used on this page: <run> is one PET file without extension (sub-01_ses-1_trc-FDG_run-1_pet); <session> is the run name without run/desc entities (sub-01_ses-1_trc-FDG); <target> is a destination space (acpc or a template name).
Run order: ANAT pipeline of the dataset first (except scenarios 2 and 3), then the CT pipeline if a CT is used, then the PET pipeline. run_all_ednix.py enforces this order.

Which route does the PET take to the template?

The scenario is chosen from IhaveanANAT, anat_pet_same_space, IhaveaCT (and the record of the CT pipeline), template_PET and creat_study_template_pet. It is written in the diary and in the registration record (coreg2anat.scenario; diagram: Illustration/pet4.svg).

1v1 · MRI, same scanner space (hybrid PET-MR)

No registration: the PET receives the world transform of the anatomy’s reorientation, is brought back to the head position of the frames acquired with the anatomy, then follows the anatomy’s AC-PC matrix and anatomy→template chain. Atlases in PET space: yes (subject atlases).

IhaveanANAT=True, anat_pet_same_space=True

1v2a · MRI, other session

Rigid PET→anatomy (mutual information, multi-start, dilated brain mask), then anatomy→template. Atlases: yes.

IhaveanANAT=True, anat_pet_same_space=False

1v2b · MRI + CT (PET/CT scanner)

PET→CT (same space: CT world transform + CT head position; otherwise rigid with head mask) → anatomy (CT pipeline) → template. Atlases: yes.

IhaveanANAT=True, IhaveaCT=True

2v1 · CT + CT template, no MRI

PET→CT→CT template (SyNRA). Atlases: template atlases.

IhaveanANAT=False, IhaveaCT=True, template_CT in the CT launcher

2v2 · CT, no CT template

PET→CT→CT study template; the group step bridge links CT study template → PET study template → reference template. Atlases: yes, through the bridge (ct_template_bridge=True).

creat_study_template_ct=True in the CT launcher

3v1 · PET template in reference space

PET→PET template (SyNRA). Atlases: template atlases.

template_PET='…'

3v2 · nothing else

PET→PET study template; the group step bridge registers the reference template to the PET study template, so every session gets the template atlases and exports in templates/<reference>, as with an anatomy (pet_template_bridge=True). With pet_template_bridge=False: study template only, no atlas (bratio only with a manual brain mask).

creat_study_template_pet=True

Same scanner space: two conditions

(1) Header: 3drefit -orient sets each origin’s sign from the orientation code, so two images of one scanner space with different fields of view can be shifted differently; the PET therefore receives the reference’s world transform W = A_after·A_before⁻¹ instead of being relabelled on its own. (2) Head position: motion correction moves the session to the mean head position; the record brings it back to the mean position of the frames in same_space_window. When in doubt, use 1v2a.

reorient.reference_world_map · MoCo.scanner_anchor

Where EDNiX writes PET files

Tools/getpath.pet() (shared with CT through _emission_tree) defines the tree below each session’s pet folder (diagram: Illustration/pet2b.svg).

<data_path>/pet/ BIDS raw images (input) + diary + Prepro_Settings.json ├── raw/ <run>_desc-frametiming_pet.json, <run>_desc-mean_pet.nii.gz, <run>_pet.sif │ └── masks/ <session>_desc-manual_mask.nii.gz (optional, drawn by hand, never deleted) ├── orig/ native PET space after reorientation │ ├── preprocessing/ desc-reorient, desc-MoCo-within, desc-MoCo-between (+ -mean), anatomy in PET space │ │ └── forMC/ crop box, per-frame matrices (_mcNNN_0GenericAffine.mat), motion .tsv/.png │ ├── matrices/ <session>_desc-registration_pet.json (record), ANTs matrices, *candidates.json, boundary check │ ├── labels/ <session>_space-orig_seg-<atlas>_dseg.nii.gz │ ├── masks/ brain / tissue masks, tissue fractions (_label-GM|WM|brain_probseg) │ └── postprocessing/ desc-activity, -SUV, -SUVglu, -bratio, -period…, -BP, -SRTM, -DVR, -PVC (+ -SS) │ └── ROI_analysis/ regional tables (.tsv), TACs (.dft), kinetic results (.res) │ └── pictures/ time-activity curves and fits ├── acpc/ parametric images in the anatomy’s AC-PC space ├── templates/<name>/ parametric images in each template space, labels/, masks/ └── QC/ PET over the brain mask of each target space, crop box, motion plots, boundary check <bids_dir>/sty_template/pet/ PET study template, its crop, reference region choice, bridge record <bids_dir>/QC/ pet_in_acpc/, pet_in_template/, pet_crop/, pet_boundary/ (all subjects)
The registration record is the contract. orig/matrices/<session>_desc-registration_pet.json lists, for every target space, its reference image, its mask and the two transform chains (to and from the PET). Steps 7–10, the CT→PET link and the group analyses all read it.

Frame timing, SIF and reorientation

modalities/pet/loop1.py + reorient.py + sifFile.py
Core stage
Why this stage exists

Raw PET headers can be oblique, mislabelled or in an animal position the scanner does not describe, and frame times in BIDS are relative to TimeZero. Step 1 fixes geometry and expresses all times after injection.

Reads / prerequisites
FILE<run>.nii.gz + .jsonFrameTimesStart, FrameDuration, InjectionStart, Units, TracerRadionuclide, PatientPosition…
SETTINGhumanPosition, animalPosition, orientation, deobliqueSame meaning as in the ANAT pipeline; one value or one per run.
SETTINGPET_header_wrongCopy the orientation code of the raw anatomy/CT of the same scanner space first.
SETTINGdcmfolder, endfiledcmOptional DICOM, to read the isotope and scan start for the SIF.
Creates / changes
FILEraw/<run>_desc-frametiming_pet.jsonFrame start/end/mid times after injection.
FILEraw/<run>_desc-mean_pet.nii.gzDuration-weighted mean of the raw frames.
FILEraw/<run>_pet.sifScan information file for the Turku tools (date 01/01/1970 without DICOM; tpcclib only displays it).
FILEorig/preprocessing/<run>_desc-reorient_pet.nii.gzReoriented PET (3D if a static image was stored as 4D with one frame).

How EDNiX creates those outputs

  1. Frame timing is read from the sidecar; overlapping frames are refused; FrameReferenceTime is used for mid-frame times when it lies inside its frame. All times become post-injection (InjectionStart).
  2. An oblique image is resampled on a cardinal grid with linear interpolation (deoblique='WARP', no overshoot of activity concentrations), then axes are relabelled from orientation or the positions.
  3. In the scanner space of the anatomy (1v1) or of the CT, the PET receives their world transform instead of its own relabelling (reorient.reference_world_map).
  4. The isotope comes from isotope, else TracerRadionuclide, else the tracer name; an unrecognised value stops the run.

What should I inspect?

Overlay the reoriented PET on the anatomy (Need_check_orient=True opens freeview). A flipped axis here cannot be fixed by any later registration.

What happens next? If a manual brain mask was drawn on the raw PET it is brought to this space; then the optional PET study template (Step 2).

Manual PET brain mask

modalities/pet/manualmask.py
Optional
Why this stage exists

Without an anatomy and an atlas (scenario 3v2) there is no brain mask in PET space, and the head box of the motion correction otherwise has to be drawn in freeview. A mask drawn once by hand on the raw PET solves both, with the same convention as func/raw/masks.

Reads / prerequisites
FILEpet/raw/masks/<session>_desc-manual_mask.nii.gzDrawn on the raw (BIDS) PET grid, before reorientation. A single *_desc-manual_mask of another name is accepted.
SETTINGuse_manual_maskTrue by default.
Creates / changes
FILEorig/masks/<session>_space-orig_desc-manual_mask.nii.gzThe mask in the reoriented PET space.

How EDNiX creates those outputs

  1. If Step 1 only rewrote the header, the voxel array of the mask is kept and receives the reoriented PET header (exact).
  2. If Step 1 resampled the PET (oblique image), the recorded world transform is applied to the mask header, then the mask is resampled on the reoriented grid (nearest neighbour).
  3. Uses: brain mask of the PET space (bratio, skull-stripped dynamic image), replacing the atlas brain; brain of the session means for the PET study template when every session has a mask (Step 2, and the 3v2 registration of Step 6); head box of crop_for_MoCo='mask' (or 'auto' without study-template crop); moving mask of pet_coreg_mask='manual'.

What should I inspect?

The reset of the PET outputs never deletes raw/masks/: the mask survives reruns.

What happens next? Step 2 (group) or Step 4.

PET study template

modalities/pet/studyT.py
creat_study_template_pet
Why this stage exists

A PET study template gives one place to draw the head crop for every session, and is the common space of scenario 3v2, linked to the reference template by the group bridge. It is not registered to the anatomy: with an anatomy, every session still goes PET → its own anatomy.

Reads / prerequisites
FILEorig/preprocessing/<run>_desc-reorient[-mean]_pet.nii.gzFirst reoriented run of every session (its mean if dynamic).
FILEorig/masks/<session>_space-orig_desc-manual_mask.nii.gzManual brain mask of every session (optional): the template is then built on the brain.
SETTINGstudyT_transform'SyNRA' by default.
Creates / changes
FILE<bids_dir>/sty_template/pet/…trc-<tracer>… study templateGroup template; sidecar BrainMasked: true when built on the brain.
FILEorig/preprocessing/forMC/<session>_desc-reorient-mean-brain_pet.nii.gzBrain of the session mean (manual masks only): mean × mask dilated by pet_mask_dilate_mm, cropped to its box + 5 mm, same world coordinates.
FILEorig/matrices/… session→study templateTransforms used later to bring its crop to each session.

How EDNiX creates those outputs

  1. When every session has a manual brain mask (use_manual_mask=True), each mean is first reduced to its brain (mask dilated by pet_mask_dilate_mm, cropped to its bounding box): the bed, the body, the extracerebral uptake and the edges of the field of view no longer drive the template. With masks for some sessions only, the whole field of view is used (WARNING).
  2. The first reoriented run of every session (its mean if dynamic) is intensity-normalised and iteratively registered to the evolving mean (4 iterations).
  3. SyNRA = rigid + affine + SyN stages. ANTsPy’s SyN and Affine start with a single affine stage, which missed a 12° rotation by about 7 mm on the test phantom (< 1 mm with SyNRA or linear TRSAA).

What should I inspect?

Open the template: a whole-FOV template (no manual masks) is dominated by the body and the bed. With creat_study_template_pet=False, a crop of a template left on disk by an earlier run is not used.

What happens next? Step 3 (crop on the study template).

Motion correction within each dynamic run

modalities/pet/MoCo.py
Core stage
Why this stage exists

Head motion during a dynamic acquisition blurs the image and corrupts time-activity curves. Frames are realigned rigidly before anything else is computed.

Reads / prerequisites
FILEorig/preprocessing/<run>_desc-reorient_pet.nii.gzReoriented dynamic run.
FILEsession crop boxFrom Step 3, the manual mask, or drawn now.
SETTINGnb_void, MoCo_transform, coreg_metricEarly frames not registered; rigid transform only (Rigid, QuickRigid, DenseRigid, BOLDRigid, Translation); Mattes MI.
Creates / changes
FILEorig/preprocessing/<run>_desc-MoCo-within_pet.nii.gz (+ -mean)Motion-corrected run.
FILEorig/preprocessing/forMC/*_mcNNN_0GenericAffine.matOne rigid matrix per frame.
FILEforMC/*_motion.tsv + .png (copied to pet/QC)Translations, rotations and mean displacement of a sphere of the head radius.
FILEpet/QC/<session>_desc-cropbox_pet.pngCrop box over the image; warning if it misses the activity.

How EDNiX creates those outputs

  1. Every frame is registered (ANTs, mutual information) to the duration-weighted mean of the frames from nb_void on, in two passes: the second uses the mean of the frames corrected by the first, because a mean of misaligned frames is a blurred reference (as SPM Realign or AFNI 3dvolreg with a mean base).
  2. The metric is computed in a head mask of the reference (voxels above 10 % of its 99th percentile, dilated 2 voxels), at three levels ending at full resolution with every voxel sampled. Without that mask the background dominates the MI histogram: on a still phantom, frames received 0.4–2 mm of spurious motion (0.2–0.4 mm with it; tests/unit/test_pet.py).
  3. Registration uses the cropped frames; the transform is applied to the full frame with linear interpolation.
  4. The first nb_void frames (low counts, different tracer distribution) receive the transform of the first registered frame.

What should I inspect?

Read the motion plot: motion before the first registered frame is not corrected. Static runs skip this step.

What happens next? Step 5 brings all runs of the session together.

modalities/pet/MoCo.py
Core stage
Why this stage exists

A session can contain several runs (or several static images). They are put in one geometry so that one registration per session is enough.

Reads / prerequisites
FILEStep-4 outputs (or reoriented static runs)If Step 4 was skipped and never run, frames are used uncorrected (warning).

modalities/pet/coreg2anat.py + boundary.py
Core stage
Why this stage exists

This stage links the PET to its target spaces according to the scenario, checks the result, and brings atlases, masks, tissue fractions and the anatomy into the PET space.

Reads / prerequisites

FILEanatomy (AC-PC head image, brain, masks, atlases) or CT record or templateTarget, by scenario.

Creates / changes
RECORDorig/matrices/<session>_desc-registration_pet.jsonScenario, targets, transform chains to/from PET.
FILEorig/matrices/<session>_from-pet-to-T1w_0GenericAffine.mat …ANTs matrices.
FILEorig/matrices/*candidates.jsonMI score and brain coverage of every start.
FILEorig/matrices/<session>_desc-registration_boundary.json/.pngBoundary check; copies in pet/QC and <bids_dir>/QC/pet_boundary.
FILEorig/labels/<session>_space-orig_seg-<atlas>_dseg.nii.gzAtlases in PET space (every level).
FILEorig/masks/<session>_space-orig[_desc-gray|white|csf]_mask.nii.gzMasks in PET space.
FILEorig/masks/<session>_space-orig_label-GM|WM|brain_probseg.nii.gzTissue fractions for PVC.
FILEorig/preprocessing/<session>_space-orig_T1w.nii.gzAnatomy in PET space.
FILEpet/QC/<session>_space-<target>_pet.pngPET in each target space with that space’s brain mask as contour (also <bids_dir>/QC/pet_in_acpc, pet_in_template).

How EDNiX creates those outputs

  1. Multi-start initialisation. ANTs starts from the centres of mass of the two images; with the torso in the PET field of view that start can be centimetres away and MI converges to a wrong optimum (85 mm error on the phantom). EDNiX tries the masked centres of mass (Alpert et al. 1990), the centres of the fields of view, the anatomy’s AC-PC matrix, identity and a rotation search (antsAffineInitializer, 15° steps within ±90°); each is followed by translation then rigid registration, and the start with the highest Mattes MI (Wells et al. 1996; Maes et al. 1997) among those covering 90 % of the brain is kept (multi-start against MI local optima: Jenkinson & Smith 2001).
  2. Masks and targets. pet_coreg_mask='anat' re-estimates the registration on the PET brain only, removing extracerebral uptake (temporal muscles, Harderian/lacrimal glands: strong in FDG, large in macaques and rodents). coreg_target='synthetic' registers to an FDG-like image of the anatomy (GM 1, WM 0.35, CSF 0.05; GM/WM ≈ 3, Phelps et al. 1979) smoothed to the PET resolution (Ashburner & Friston 1997); FDG only. Similarity/Affine can absorb a misregistration with a scale and non-linear transforms are not physiological within a subject (warnings).
  3. Boundary check. The PET in AC-PC space is averaged in 0.5-mm shells of signed distance to the brain boundary, in six sectors, and compared with the expected profile of the FDG-like anatomy. Sectors with uptake 3–5 mm outside the brain that the brain does not explain are left out; from the clean sectors, the median half-maximum shift is a dilation (scale error) and half the difference of opposite sectors a translation. Below the tolerance (largest PET voxel, ≥ 1 mm; retrospective PET-MRI accuracy is about 2 mm, West et al. 1997) the registration is accepted and a dim outer cortex is partial volume (Hoffman et al. 1979; Rousset et al. 1998).
  4. Resolution estimate. The FDG-like image is smoothed at 1–10 mm and the FWHM that best correlates with the registered PET is kept (fwhm_estimated_mm; idea from Joshi et al. 2009). Approximate on real data; used by pvc_fwhm='auto'.
  5. Into PET space. Atlases: generic-label interpolation (every level of a 4D atlas); masks: linear interpolation thresholded at 0.5; tissue fractions: averaged over the extent of a PET voxel before linear resampling. With an anatomy, the subject’s atlases (including manual corrections) are used; without, the template’s.

What should I inspect?

Look at every space-<target>_pet.png and at the boundary verdict. A cortex whose FDG spreads symmetrically into CSF and WM is partial volume, not misregistration; a misregistration shifts the signal into CSF on one side and WM on the other. On phantoms all starts, masks and the synthetic target reach < 0.4 mm; real data will do worse.

What happens next? In 2v2 and 3v2, the group bridge; otherwise Step 7. In 3v2 with a brain template (Step 2), the brain of the motion-corrected session mean (<session>_desc-MoCo-mean-brain_pet.nii.gz) is registered to it.

Link the study template to the reference template

modalities/pet/bridge.py
ct_template_bridge · pet_template_bridge
Why this stage exists

A CT has almost no contrast inside the brain, so a CT study template cannot be registered to a T1 template. An FDG group mean has grey/white contrast and can. In 3v2 (no CT, no anatomy) the PET study template is linked the same way, so that atlases and template-space outputs exist as with an anatomy.

Reads / prerequisites
RECORDevery 2v2 / 3v2 session record (Steps 1–6 done)PET → CT → CT study template chains (2v2) or PET → PET study template (3v2).
FILEskull-stripped reference template + brain maskMoving image of the bridge.
SETTINGbridge_transform'Affine' (default) or 'SyNRA'.
Creates / changes
RECORD<bids_dir>/sty_template/pet/matrices/studyTemplate_to_<reference>_desc-bridge_pet.jsonBridge record; Skip_step=['bridge'] reuses it.
FILEPET study template in CT study-template space + <study template>_space-<reference>_pet.pngTemplate and its QC image.
SIDE EFFECTeach session record extendedReference target and template atlases/masks added in PET space.

How EDNiX creates those outputs

  1. 2v2: PET study template = mean of the session means brought to the CT study template through their own chains, each scaled by its 99th percentile (dose). A mean, not ants.build_template, whose extra warps would not be part of the session chains.
  2. Reference template → PET study template: multi-start similarity, then affine (the Translation / Rigid / Similarity / Affine cascade of ANTs TRSAA); SyNRA adds SyN (Avants et al. 2008) in the dilated FDG mask.
  3. 3v2: no step 1, the PET study template of Step 2 (brain template with manual masks) is already the space of the sessions.
  4. Every 2v2 / 3v2 session then receives the reference target and atlases; Steps 7–10 run as in 2v1 and Step 10 also writes templates/<reference>. A run mixing 2v2 and 3v2 sessions is refused.

What should I inspect?

This is a between-subject registration of templates of different modalities: check the QC image before using the atlases. Phantom: chain within 2 mm, Dice > 0.7 of a template region.

modalities/pet/preproPET.py + paramImg.py + roi_signal.py
Core stage
Why this stage exists

Turns the motion-corrected PET into quantitative images and regional tables, in PET space.

Reads / prerequisites
FILEStep-5 motion-corrected run / session meanActivity data.
FILEPET-space atlases, brain mask, manual maskFrom Step 6.
SETTINGSUV, bratio, SUV_window, Do_cut, duration, selected_atlasesWhich images and tables.
Creates / changes

FILE…_desc-SUV[-mean], -SUVgluBody-weight SUV and glucose-normalised SUV.
FILE…_desc-bratio[-mean], -bratio-period…Ratio to the brain mean; time blocks of Do_cut.
FILE… -SS versionsSkull-stripped.
FILEROI_analysis/*_seg-<atlas>-lvl<k>_vol_ROIs.tsv, *_desc-<desc>_ROIs.tsvRegional mean, SD, median, voxels, volume per atlas and level.
FILEROI_analysis/pictures/Regional time-activity curves of dynamic runs.

How EDNiX creates those outputs

  1. SUV (g/mL) = C (Bq/mL) × body weight (g) / injected radioactivity (Bq), density 1 g/mL (Boellaard 2009; Boellaard et al. 2015; Kinahan & Fletcher 2010). Injected activity is decayed to ImageDecayCorrectionTime when it differs from InjectionStart; non-decay-corrected data are decay-corrected frame by frame (exact frame-averaged factor).
  2. SUVglu = SUV × glucose / 5.0 mmol/L (GLUCOSE_REF_MMOL_L; other protocols use 5.55); optional in clinical guidelines.
  3. bratio = image / its mean over the brain mask (the manual mask when present).
  4. Units are converted to Bq/mL from the sidecar (Bq/mL, kBq/cc, nCi/mL, DICOM BQML…); an image already in SUV is kept.

What should I inspect?

Regional values include partial-volume effects: interpret small regions (and small brains) with caution, or use the pvc step. Check Prepro_Settings.json for the source of weight and dose.

srtm=True
Why this stage exists

For receptor tracers, binding potential and distribution volume ratio are estimated without blood sampling, from a reference region.

SETTINGreference_signalAtlas labels, a tissue mask (white, csf, white+csf, gray, brain) or 'unknown' (interactive, saved for the next subjects).

Creates / changes
FILEROI_analysis/*_desc-Kinetic_ROIs.tsv/.dft, reference .dft, BPnd .resRegional results.

FILE<bids_dir>/sty_template/pet/reference_region_trc-<tracer>.jsonSaved reference choice.

How EDNiX creates those outputs

  1. Regional BPnd: SRTM (Lammertsma & Hume 1996) fitted with basis functions (bfmsrtm, Gunn et al. 1997), frame weights from imgweigh/dftweigh (Mazoyer et al. 1986).
  2. Voxel-wise BPnd: imgbfbp and imgsrtm. Voxel-wise DVR: Logan plot with reference (imgdv, Logan et al. 1996) from DV_time, with -k2= when DV_k2ref is given, otherwise the simplified form.
  3. Regional and reference TACs are computed on the motion-corrected (not skull-stripped) image, to avoid diluting TACs with zeros; voxel-wise models on the skull-stripped version. Frame times given to the tools are post-injection.

What should I inspect?

The validity of a reference region (no specific binding, same non-displaceable uptake) depends on tracer and species: check the literature for each tracer. Prefer label numbers to a name pattern ('Cerebell' can also match cerebellar WM).

Partial-volume correction

modalities/pet/pvc.py
pvc_fwhm
Why this stage exists

A cortex thinner than the PET resolution loses signal to CSF and white matter. PVC restores tissue concentrations given the scanner resolution.

Reads / prerequisites
FILEfirst static result (SUV-mean, SUV, activity-mean or activity)Image to correct.
FILEtissue fractions (Step 6)Four classes summing to one: GM, WM outside it, CSF = rest of the brain mask, rest.
SETTINGpvc_fwhm, pvc_method, pvc_enginePSF FWHM in mm (not the voxel size; 'auto' = Step-6 estimate, never below ReconFilterSize); RBV default; 'auto' engine.
Creates / changes
FILEpostprocessing/…_desc-<desc>-PVC_pet.nii.gz + JSONCorrected image; the JSON records engine and GTM class means.

How EDNiX creates those outputs

  1. pvc_engine='auto': PETPVC (Thomas et al. 2016) through nipype when petpvc is on the PATH, otherwise the Python implementation (RBV and MG only).
  2. Python definitions: GTM (Rousset et al. 1998) with tissue fractions as weights; RBV (Thomas et al. 2011) = PET × S / (PSF ⊗ S); MG (Müller-Gärtner et al. 1992).
  3. GTM itself writes a table, not an image, and is refused.

What should I inspect?

On the folded-cortex phantom RBV brings pure GM from 0.70–0.82 back to 0.97–0.99 of its true value (noise-free, exact PSF). On real data a wrong pvc_fwhm and noise limit it: prefer the scanner’s value when known.

What happens next? Step 9 or Step 10.

What the PET pipeline reads from BIDS

Layout: sub-<ID>/ses-<S>/pet/sub-<ID>_ses-<S>_trc-<tracer>[_run-<n>]_pet.nii.gz + sidecar (Norgaard et al. 2022). Selected by endpet.

FieldUse
FrameTimesStart, FrameDuration (s)Frame timing (required for dynamic; overlapping frames refused).
InjectionStart (s, relative to TimeZero)Origin of every time in the pipeline: SUV_window, duration, DV_time, kinetic_end, same_space_window are post-injection.
UnitsConversion to Bq/mL for SUV.
InjectedRadioactivity, InjectedRadioactivityUnitsSUV.
ImageDecayCorrected, ImageDecayCorrectionTimeDecay reference; non-corrected data are corrected at Step 7.
TracerRadionuclideIsotope (F18, 18F, [18F], Fluorine-18 recognised).
TimeZeroSIF title line; PET-fMRI timing.
PatientPosition, PatientWeighthumanPosition when empty; body weight when not in the TSV files.
ReconFilterType, ReconFilterSizeReported with PVC; lower bound of pvc_fwhm='auto'.
Body weight and blood glucose are read from sub-<ID>_sessions.tsv, then participants.tsv (body_weight/weight/weight_kg in kg; blood_glucose in mmol/L or blood_concentration in g/L; injected dose also as injected_radioactivity or tracor_qte_MBq in MBq).

Interactive steps and unattended runs

  • Need_check_orient, Need_check_MC, Need_check_coreg: freeview after reorientation, motion correction, registration.
  • do_manual=True: move the PET in freeview (Tools > Transform Volume), save under the same name; the registration is estimated again from it.
  • crop_for_MoCo: crops are saved and reused; redo_crop=True redraws them.
  • reference_signal='unknown': a window to choose the reference region, saved for the next subjects.
  • For unattended runs (server, interface in the background): crop_for_MoCo='none' or 'mask', an explicit reference_signal, checks off, and EDNIX_STRICT=1.

Limitations to check on the first datasets

  • Same scanner space: the anatomy/CT must not have been moved by hand after reorientation, and same_space_window must match its acquisition.
  • Turku tools write outputs back with the input geometry: check one parametric image against the anatomy.
  • Scenarios 2v2 and 3v2: the bridge is a T1 → FDG-mean registration of templates; check <study template>_space-<reference>_pet.png before using atlases. 3v2 with pet_template_bridge=False: no atlas.
  • Very early frames inherit the transform of the first registered frame.
  • Registration accuracies quoted here come from noise-free or Gaussian-noise phantoms with 2-mm voxels; real data (lower counts, changing contrast between frames) will do worse.
